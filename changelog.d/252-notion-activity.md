Added
- Notion activity as a per-person weekly signal (#252). An opt-in `notion:` config block (`token: "${NOTION_TOKEN}"`, redacted in `Debug` and `Serialize`) makes `tga collect` store `page_created`, `page_edited` (last editor only, a lower bound) and `comment_created` (body length only) events in the new `fact_notion_activity` table, bucketed by ISO week, with actors resolved to canonical emails through the identity resolver. Migration v40 adds `fact_notion_activity` and `notion_sync_cursor`. A failed fetch or write records a stage failure and leaves the cursor unchanged. Notion activity is never summed into effort.
